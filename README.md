This project Reveals the Total number of Athlete involved within the reporting period, Maximum and Minimum Training Hours and Training Hour trends of Various Athlete's, Analyzed using Microsoft Excel. 

## Project Highlihgts
- This project shows that 1000 Athlete's participated in the Sports.
- This project Reveals Athlete's I.D with the top 10 training Hours.
- This project explain Athlete's Multi-evaluation Trends.

  ## Key Insights
  - Athletics sport as the Highest record of Arthletes of 146.
  - Athletics sport as the Highest record of Injury risk of 145.
  - The dashboard shows the highest training hours of Athletes training to be 6hrs.
  - The Dashboard shows that most athletes are having high fat

## Recommendations
- The athletes with the highest training hours should be monitored so as not to drop while those with poor training hours should be followed up in other to meetup. 
- For those Athlete with fatigue issues should pay attention to there health to avoid sudden health breakdown.
- Risk management scheme should be consider to help manage and reduce risk of Athletes during Sporting.
- Individual training plan should be created base on each athletes condition and there choosen sports.
- Nutrition level of all athletes should be maintain and sustained in other to continue to keep them feated.
